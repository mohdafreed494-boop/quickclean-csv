Help & Privacy Support

How is my data kept private?

QuickClean processes all files directly in your web browser using JavaScript FileReader, PapaParse, and SheetJS. Files are never transmitted across the network, stored on an external database, or seen by any third party.

What formats are supported?

You can upload Microsoft Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx, .xls) as well as comma-separated values (.csv). Outputs are exported as clean, standard CSV or JSON files.

Privacy Policy

QuickClean Business Data Toolkit is engineered with a strict zero-storage, privacy-first policy.

  • No Cloud Uploads: When you upload a CSV or Excel file, it is parsed strictly in your browser's local RAM.
  • No Analytics Tracking: We do not track or sell your business records, spreadsheets, or identity.
  • Session Discard: As soon as you refresh or close this tab, all in-memory data is instantly wiped.
